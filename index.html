The Destination

A stop worth pulling off for.

Rangeway Mojave brings a new kind of arrival to the CA-14 corridor. It is a high-desert place to recharge, reset, and head back out with more of the day ahead.

The best roadside places have always offered more than a reason to stop. They give the road a little character.

Mojave is planned as part of a larger regional travel plaza, with food, retail, traveler services, and room to spend a little time. The goal is simple: build something that feels like a destination, not another parking lot.

The charging plaza is the Rangeway experience within that larger place. Comfortable, useful, and designed around the person who just got out of the car.

The Rangeway Standard

We think like hotel operators, not utility companies.

In the high desert, indoor comfort is not an amenity. It is the product. Rangeway Mojave is designed around a simple hospitality standard: every driver should leave feeling better than when they arrived.

The Energy

Built for a long way from the grid.

Mojave is planned as an integrated solar, storage, and ultra-fast charging platform, designed for reliable power in one of America’s strongest solar-resource areas.

Sunlight

A dedicated solar field is planned to capture the high desert’s most abundant resource and make it part of every stop.

Storage

Battery energy storage will hold that energy and manage the load that comes with a busy charging plaza.

Charging

Ultra-fast DC charging will bring the system’s stored energy directly to drivers, with no utility-company experience attached.

Phase I

The first stop starts here.

Phase I pairs charging with the essentials for a good stop: shade, food, comfortable seating, and a climate-controlled place to come in from the heat.

Charging under the canopy

An open-bay charging plaza, designed to feel like pulling up to a property instead of a parking lot.

A driver's lounge

A climate-controlled place to cool off, settle in, and return to the road feeling ready for what is next.

Food and a little room to stay

Outdoor seating and food service will make a charging stop feel like part of the trip, not time spent waiting for it.
